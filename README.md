# AROMAFLOW – Smart Solar-Powered Agarbatti Drying System

**A Smart, Solar-Powered Drying and Compact Packaging System for Home-Based Agarbatti Manufacturing by Rural Women Artisans.**

## Project Overview

AROMAFLOW is a smart, compact, and energy-efficient agarbatti drying system designed to support rural women artisans, self-help groups (SHGs), and small-scale home-based incense stick manufacturers.

The system utilizes solar energy to provide heat within an insulated drying chamber, creating a controlled environment for efficient and consistent agarbatti drying. It integrates IoT-based monitoring to track temperature and humidity and regulate ventilation according to drying conditions.

The proposed system is designed to accommodate approximately **3–5 kg of agarbatti per batch**, reducing dependence on traditional open-air drying methods. It also incorporates eco-friendly kraft paper packaging to support sustainable manufacturing practices.

## Problem Statement

Traditional agarbatti manufacturers, particularly rural women artisans and home-based businesses, largely depend on open-air and sunlight-based drying methods.

These methods present several challenges:

- **Weather Dependency:** Rainfall, humidity, and insufficient sunlight affect drying.
- **Inconsistent Drying:** Uneven moisture levels can impact product quality.
- **Time Consumption:** Traditional drying requires considerable time.
- **Manual Effort:** Continuous monitoring and handling increase workload.
- **Product Wastage:** Improper drying can lead to damaged or unusable products.
- **High Equipment Costs:** Conventional industrial drying equipment may be unaffordable for small-scale manufacturers.

AROMAFLOW aims to address these challenges through an affordable, solar-powered, and intelligent drying solution.

## Proposed Solution

AROMAFLOW consists of two primary chambers:

### 1. Outer Solar Heating Chamber
- Designed to capture and retain solar heat.
- Uses insulating materials to minimize heat loss.
- Incorporates an adjustable reflector to improve solar heat collection.

### 2. Inner Drying Chamber
- Insulated chamber placed inside the outer enclosure.
- Equipped with 3–4 removable drying trays.
- Designed for approximately 3–5 kg of agarbatti per batch.
- Uses controlled airflow to support uniform drying.
- Includes an exhaust fan for ventilation and moisture removal.

### 3. Smart Monitoring System
- Temperature and humidity sensors monitor internal conditions.
- ESP32 processes sensor readings.
- IoT-based monitoring provides information about drying conditions.
- Automated ventilation helps regulate airflow.

## Working Principle

1. **Solar Energy Collection:** The solar panel supplies electrical energy to the system, while the outer chamber is designed to collect solar heat.
2. **Heat Generation:** The outer chamber collects solar heat and transfers it towards the inner drying chamber.
3. **Agarbatti Placement:** Freshly manufactured agarbatti sticks are arranged on removable trays inside the drying chamber.
4. **Environmental Monitoring:** Temperature and humidity sensors continuously monitor the internal drying conditions.
5. **Smart Ventilation:** The ESP32 controls the exhaust fan based on monitored conditions to regulate airflow.
6. **Drying Process:** Agarbatti sticks undergo controlled drying to achieve more consistent drying conditions.
7. **Packaging:** After drying, the finished agarbatti is prepared for eco-friendly kraft paper packaging.
